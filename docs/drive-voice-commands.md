# Drive voice commands (standalone web)

Supersedes the inert AI toggle described in drive-voice-preview.md. No native/main change.

Tap AI, review the privacy notice once per drive and agree. The accepted trip ID is stored per account in sessionStorage (in-memory fallback if storage is blocked). This never auto-enables the microphone. Activation speaks an audible greeting directly from the tap to establish playback. Replies use the selected Voice Preference. Missing speech events time out visibly instead of leaving the control stuck.

Voice stays enabled without a 60-second cap. It alternates command recognition and speech with a 650ms restart delay; ordinary silence restarts recognition. While speaking, a separate best-effort recognizer accepts only stop talking/stop speaking/be quiet/hold on/wait/listen to me, optionally prefixed with Hey Cubo. It stops playback and resumes command listening; it NEVER executes a hazard, route, or off confirmation during playback. It rejects phrases contained in its own response to reduce echo and permits at most two recognition restarts per reply. Unsupported simultaneous recognition/TTS does not retry indefinitely; tap AI stops immediately. Safari may reject or disrupt simultaneous audio. This is not guaranteed full-duplex speech on every phone. Permission/network/audio errors, three immediate command recognition endings, screen/account/trip changes, hiding the app and attention warnings turn it off. There is no background listening.

Say Hey Cubo before a new question. A 20-second ordinary follow-up window accepts questions without the prefix. Pending confirmations and repair phrases such as respond bypass that wake-word window. The confirmation window is armed for 60 seconds AFTER speech finishes; an initial 120-second bound covers speech setup. Other idle transcripts are discarded without a response. This is browser transcription followed by phrase matching, NOT a low-power on-device wake-word engine: audio, including surrounding conversation, may be processed by the browser provider while enabled. No new model is loaded.

Tap the enabled icon to turn off immediately. Spoken off requests ask for yes/no or confirm, without needing Hey Cubo. Pending requests survive repeated prompts, other questions and toggling voice within their time limit. The command memory belongs to the account's active drive provider, not each microphone activation. New accounts/drives reset it. A new action replaces the old pending action; confirmation is single-use and cannot confirm both a hazard and voice-off.

Examples: my selected vehicle; who are my kids; Child 1 score; and their score; my insurance; my home address; my name; next turn; how fast am I going; how am I doing this drive; how long have I driven; monitoring status; repeat that; stop listening.

Hazards: report debris/object, police, or crash; then confirm or cancel. Bound to the original trip and fresh original GPS position. Reports are trip events only, not published to the community. Other mutations, including ending a drive, are not supported. Family data is explicitly demo data. Missing measurements are never replaced with an invented score or turn.

Navigation: destination comes from the selected route; upcoming instructions use the map's current step index. And after that/what then advances a separate spoken preview cursor, never changes navigation. Remaining time/distance are approximated from position projected onto route geometry and the original route duration/distance. No live traffic or precise arrival guarantee. Off-route positions over 100m from geometry or stale/weak GPS do not produce ETA.

Reroute/alternate routes/missed my turn explicitly fetch up to two usable OSRM alternatives from current GPS to the selected destination. Existing routing already uses this public service: these requests send origin/destination coordinates, use a 10-second timeout, and throttle repeated fetches to once per 15 seconds. The service can return one route only, fail, or rate-limit; no availability guarantee or paid service is introduced. Road labels and approximate minutes come from returned steps/durations, never invented. Say option one/two, then confirm or cancel. Confirmations check account-bound control lifetime, same trip/destination, fresh GPS, age under two minutes and movement under 150 meters. Stale choices require another request. Switching resets navigation step/following, preserves the active trip, and does not discard recorded route/events. Arbitrary via-road, toll/highway avoidance, traffic and speed-limit questions return honest limitations.

Drive responses are capped at 240 characters on complete sentences (route choice menus are separately bounded to two labels). Broad voice-off variants bypass chat help. Unsupported commands use short drive-specific responses. Chat responses themselves are unchanged. The phone web shell requests portrait lock and adds orientation to the PWA manifest. On browsers rejecting lock, physical landscape displays an upright-use notice rather than desktop preview chrome; content remains mounted. Keyboard opening does not trigger the notice. Native Expo already declares portrait orientation and is unchanged.

No WebLLM/model invocation, new dependencies, chat quota consumption or audio recording. Accepted user questions and generated replies are appended to the account-scoped local drive checkpoint. The transcript link appears below Drive events and in Activity trip details. It is not sent in the family-sharing payload or uploaded as Firebase chat data. Deleting a trip removes its transcript; transcripts can also be deleted independently. Clearing browser data loses local transcripts. Consent v3 discloses this changed storage behavior.

Transcript storage stops (without deleting previous turns) at 100,000 serialized characters per trip, roughly 200KB of UTF-16 text plus storage overhead. Individual accepted commands are limited to 1,000 characters and stored turns to 1,200. A visible summary notice marks a limited transcript; storage failures use the existing checkpoint error. Limits do not stop voice commands. Normal short exchanges are much smaller. Total history still uses device storage, so users may need to delete old transcripts/trips. This saves accepted conversation text, not ignored ambient speech or proof of audible playback. Whole-drive text history is available for review and recent-question recall, while contextual command handling remains preset-based—not unrestricted language understanding. Browser/device support, permissions, noise, battery and memory costs vary; no all-phone guarantee.

Verify on a parked phone: hear the activation greeting; allow microphone; ask vehicle/kids/score and verify speech; ask a second question after the reply; wait over 60 seconds then say Hey Cubo and another question; confirm/cancel a hazard and inspect Activity; test spoken stop with yes/no, manual off/on without another notice, a new drive with a new notice, denied permission and app backgrounding. Do not interact with the phone while driving. Automated command/lifecycle tests do not certify iPhone microphone/audio behavior. Persistent recognition consumes more battery/network than the former 60-second sessions; runtime costs have not been measured on the user's phone.
